company.json

Make sure AI gets your company right.

AI is becoming a primary way people learn about a company — and it often gets the basics wrong: an outdated logo, the wrong contact email, a social account nobody monitors anymore. company.json gives AI systems, and anyone else, one official place to find the real answer: your name, official site, logo, contacts, brands, and more — published on your own domain, controlled by you.
